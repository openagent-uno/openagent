# Connector identities

Official provider logos identify their integrations in the local catalog. Originals,
retrieval dates and SHA-256 hashes are listed in `sources.json`. Trademarks remain
the property of their respective owners. They do not imply endorsement.

PNG assets are loaded locally; SVG originals are also encoded in
`features/agents/connector-vectors.ts` for React Native SVG (no remote requests).
IMAP/SMTP and custom MCP are protocols, so they use the shared mail/link icons.
